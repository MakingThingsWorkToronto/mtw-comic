# Content communication rubric

Assess every ordinary page and client-facing shared surface, including recently completed copy. Record **meets**, **revise**, **evidence needed**, or **not applicable with reason**; use no score, length quota or required number of examples. A retention needs a reason, just as a rewrite does. This rubric applies the [content rules](content.md), [positioning](positioning.md), [experience boundaries](../experience/measured-outcomes.md) and approved visual composition. It creates no new offer, section or publication permission.

| Test | What to establish |
|---|---|
| Buyer concern | The reader recognizes a problem, task or decision at their AI starting point. Lead with the work, using technical detail when it helps that decision. |
| Consequence | Explain what changes and why it matters. Distinguish an intended benefit from a delivered result; avoid generic transformation claims and guarantees. |
| Relevant evidence | Support each factual claim with an attested project, working system, appropriate primary source or clearly identified illustration. Preserve scope, dates, estimates, denominators and permission. A number is useful when supported and relevant; it is not compulsory. |
| Fit and scope | Show why Matt's judgement and combination of secure foundations, maintained knowledge and practical agent work fit this concern. Make the available help and continuing human responsibility understandable without publishing the entire method. |
| Clarity and proportion | Make one plain point, support it, then stop. Preserve approved headings and composition. Tour text explains the work and its significance, not the animation. Leave room for the reader's next question without implying something untrue. |
| Useful next action | Links describe their destination and controls describe what happens. A demo request begins a conversation; a static viewer is not a tour; sending a submission onward is not verified inbox receipt. Do not add a CTA to every paragraph. |
| Consistency and access | Keep the same claim and service meaning in page prose, cards, captions, accessible text, metadata, tour summaries and templates, and agent-readable output. Discover every consumer of changed shared copy. Natural service terms and authorized service areas support findability; no ranking guarantee follows. |

Unsupported claims, identifying client details, invented results, misleading actions, unfinished public copy and unauthorized alterations of signed text require correction or a named evidence dependency. Other strengths cannot compensate. Keep all prices off the public site. Public copy must not expose internal source notes or review instructions.

Use claim states deliberately: **attested result**, **implemented behavior** (with its actual verification limit), **documented product capability**, **proposed service approach**, **illustrative scenario**, or **attributed forecast/opinion**. An Azure move delivered in two weeks against a six-week estimate is not a general threefold speed gain. Task Field adoption shares and animation scale do not measure savings. A connector configured for search does not establish a Copilot interaction. Keep these boundaries wherever the claim recurs, without repeating internal audit language to visitors.

The signed LLM Journal is assessed for faithful text, attribution, supporting context, factual concerns and presentation. Preserve each author's words, stance and historical perspective. Report a proposed factual correction through the maintained column workflow; do not silently rewrite signed passages into the service-page voice. The ordinary Journal's interview/drafting method remains separate from this site-wide communication rubric.

## Lessons from retained competitor research

These are communication lessons from five public-offer reports researched on 15 September 2026, not a current market ranking or proof of absent capabilities. Their original access limits remain relevant. Refresh a dated primary source before publishing a changing product or comparative assertion.

| Report | Apply | Do not import |
|---|---|---|
| [Taiga AI](../competitors/taiga-ai.md), §§2–4, 9 | Make the engagement and buyer decision recognizable; consider human review when explaining a useful result. | Governance-only shorthand: its retained blog research also discusses workflow redesign and verified outcomes. |
| [Creospark](../competitors/creospark.md), §§2–5, 9 | Concrete scope and recognizable business concerns make an offer assessable. | Prices, free assessments, compulsory FAQs, or a claim that competitors do not build agents or forecast consumption. |
| [2toLead](../competitors/2tolead.md), §§4–5, 9 | Use proof relevant to the particular service; explain ownership and practical failure modes. | Unrelated statistics as service proof, or lack of a found public case as proof of no delivery. |
| [Empathy Technologies](../competitors/empathy-technologies.md), §§1, 4, 9 | Concrete migration concerns communicate more than a generic benefits list; claims must agree across surfaces. | Its ratings, savings, self-ranking or pricing; none establishes an MTW result or warrants denigration. |
| [Centric Consulting](../competitors/centric-consulting.md), §§3–5, 9 | Connect the problem, implemented work and relevant outcome; make ongoing ownership understandable. | Its unit-cost, ROI or speed benchmarks, free offers, named phases or a governance-only characterization. |

Establish MTW's position positively with supported examples. This research does not authorize public prices, exhaustive methods, new sections, industry pages, fixed packages, copied proof, exclusivity claims or a change from audience starting points to company-size targeting.

## Review record

Record the source hash and location, reader concern, claim state and evidence, criterion finding, rewrite or reasoned retention, shared consumers and placement checks, dependencies, and checks actually performed. Assess shared wording once and link that record to every placement; assess the surrounding promise and fit at each placement. Noindex pages remain in scope. Apply the existing mirrored content-refinement, Task Field, column and ai-slop-check workflows. Static checks, native reading, deployment and Matt's approval are distinct.
