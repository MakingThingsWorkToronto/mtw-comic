# Phone framing and device surfaces

Resolve the exact canonical master and hash before inspecting source art. Inspect every drawn panel, retaining each key face, action hand, clock, signature and evidence object. Record the actual device screen direction: UI belongs on the front/display surface visible to its viewer. A rear housing, camera side, vent, port edge or outer laptop lid cannot carry screen UI. Holographic interfaces need an explicit story basis. Keep source bytes and old versions read-only; a confirmed composition defect needs a bounded new version rather than a crop trick.

The current loader decodes one `scene.art` master on desktop and phone. `phonePages` rearranges reader boxes; `phoneArt` reserves a contained viewport for the same source panel, and is not a separate mobile image URL. Verify the actual source rectangle `ar`, responsive box and real crop window. Do not mistake low source-area retention for a defect when all important subjects remain. When a landscape source is cover-cropped into a tall phone box, try a contained phone viewport first and inspect the resulting scale and lettering. If that cannot keep story-critical content legible, author bounded responsive artwork with explicit loader/data support; merely placing another master in the library does not enable it.

For device preflight, source manifests use `scene.masterSha256` and a per-panel `deviceReview: {reviewed:true, devices:[{label,surface,uiVisible,sourcePixelRect}]}`. Surfaces are `front`, `back`, `edge` or `hologram`; a visible UI on back/edge fails. Explicit empty devices means the source panel was inspected and has no visible device. These are human source observations, not automatic pixel recognition. Each rectangle is in source pixels and must stay inside the master. New/revised artwork reviews run `comic-story-art-review.mjs --manifest <json> --require-device-audit`; older retained manifests remain readable without that flag, but do not establish this newly requested check.

Source-subject projection includes the allowed art translation/scale from the actual transformed crop rectangle; balloons, frame and paper remain still. Run `node site/scripts/comic-story-framing-regression.mjs` before the serialized native review. ## Physical screen direction after device edits

Before a device-side edit, inspect its physical front, back and viewing angle against the visible operator. A bezel alone does not establish a correct screen: its glass must face the person operating it. Keep visible UI off rear lids, camera backs, vented housings and device edges. A rear-only viewpoint stays plain, retaining a separate actual front-screen evidence shot where the page has one. A side-on view may show a foreshortened front toward the operator, with a plain larger rear housing. Do not invent a floating UI overlay to hide a physical composition error.

For new reviewed UI-bearing `front` devices, `deviceReview.devices[]` records `operatorView: "faces-screen"` or `"no-visible-operator"` from the exact master. The required gate rejects a missing declaration or `faces-away`; older optional declarations remain readable. This is a hash-bound human observation, not pixel recognition. Inspect the exact full page and targeted crop after editing, retain rejected attempts, and rebind any moved protected-subject rectangle before the final phone review.

## Phone evidence scale and actual lettering regions

Before generating a content view, bind its actual `pageArt`/`phoneArt` rectangles and the rendered contained image area. Reserve empty space inside the artwork only when fixed lettering actually overlays it; external balloons do not justify invented dark stripes. Inspect every source subject against the real mapping, while retaining any mismatch with the submitted prompt separately.

A requested font size is not an output measurement. Inspect the returned glyph height in source pixels, then estimate displayed height as `glyphHeight * min(artAreaWidth / cropWidth, artAreaHeight / cropHeight)`. Record the exact output hash, crop rectangle and area assumptions. For a 390px phone, aim for about16px body evidence lettering and verify it in the real reader; passing crop arithmetic cannot establish native legibility, balloon clearance or reading dwell. A wide content image can require separate source/procedure and checked-example/output close-ups. Keep each checked example beside the concrete retained output values, rather than substituting generic paper icons/checkmarks; never invent a rate, amount or new claim to fill the screen.
