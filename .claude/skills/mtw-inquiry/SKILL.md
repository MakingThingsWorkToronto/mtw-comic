# Comic inquiry

Read root AGENTS.md and the requested comic brief. Use the comic-story storytelling reference; no private website, Journal queue or external wiki service is required.

State the current interpretation before asking a question. Ask only what could change that interpretation: the protagonist's want, the harmful action, the visible evidence, the repair or its consequence. Reflect the answer back accurately and revise the scene requirements when it contradicts the premise. Do not supply a human's decision, fabricate approval, or turn every answer into support for an already fixed story. Record the resulting story facts with the issue's source data.
