# Comic articulation

Read root AGENTS.md, docs/authoring.md and the requested issue brief. The comic-story storytelling reference owns the story contract.

1. State the reader's starting knowledge and the scene's concrete consequence.
2. Answer what the scene says, what this reader needs to understand, and which visible action proves it. Keep one main point per beat.
3. Compress the idea into one plain sentence. A missing example or unclear consequence needs a story decision, not more adjectives.
4. Choose context, action and consequence in causal order. Establish who acts, what changes and why the next box follows.
5. Draft only the requested words. Preserve exact approved text and character identity; an invented comic event is fiction, not evidence of consulting results.
6. Read pictures and dialogue together. Remove explanation already visible in the drawing; retain facts needed to understand the harm and repair. Speech production waits for the actual words and voice authorization.
