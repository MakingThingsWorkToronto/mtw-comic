## Comic borders and margins

All comics use brand teal `#1a9ba1` for panel frames, gutters, inset separator bands and page margins, including loaders, desktop, phone and fallback views. Never use white, off-white or sampled paper for these surfaces. Keep approved balloon fills and lettering colours separate. Retain reference geometry and texture; sampling reference paper must not overwrite the branded separator. Inspect the repaired reader and the next different comic before delivery.

## Work in this order

1. **Write a story a new reader can follow.** Use [storytelling](references/storytelling.md), the existing world and Matt's actual feedback. Establish place, time, protagonist, want and trouble on page one. In the first scene’s first box, write a deliberate bottom-right teal `sceneSetup` caption naming the invented company, its actual work, the immediate trouble and the concrete deadline or consequence; a location label alone cannot establish the job. Introduce people through an identifying caption and a proving action. Each event visibly causes the next; the ending shows the consequence. Develop the requested issue scope; change a retained demo only within Matt’s explicit current authorization. Use `mtw-articulation` for changed copy and `mtw-inquiry` when a real unresolved story fact needs Matt.
2. **Read the whole script cold before art.** State what each page changes, who causes harm and who stops or corrects it. The reader must not need production notes to understand that. A quote being wrong, an email going to the wrong person and a destroyed office are different events. Keep the mentor, operators and agents' roles clear. Villains enter through damage, not a roll call. Use only invented hockey-derived names and companies, following the world bible.
3. **Bind art to those exact beats.** Extract the revised story's `r`, `alt`, balloon positions and speaker identities. Use [art pipeline](references/art-pipeline.md). One generated page per scene; its boxes are reveal regions. Fixed cast references establish identity. Verify the actual person, action, evidence object and consequence in every region; a calibration page cannot become another event through new captions.
4. **Integrate real images and readable lettering.** Use high-resolution raster page art, `scene.art` and detected `ar`, while `r` places the boxes in the responsive reader. Keep borders, gutters, balloons and lettering crisp. Register each version and its provenance in the canonical image library. Cover/NEXT images must resolve to actual art. Preserve the approved 2.1.0 balloon style and the existing full-window layout. The opening context caption uses brand teal `#1a9ba1` at the existing caption alpha `0.85`, the existing caption lettering/rule, and runtime bottom-right placement with measured height. Reserve artwork and reading time for it on desktop and phone.
5. **Check the changed outcome.** Follow [checks](references/checks.md): cheap static checks, actual image resolution, then one serialized desktop/phone review of the story with its final art and balloons. Inspect the evidence yourself. The page must tell the intended story with key subjects visible, tails pointing correctly, and every image loaded. Re-run style-lab or golden calibration only when its typography, balloons, paper or geometry changed.

## Fast path: fan out one issue within 90 minutes

Use the 90-minute target to bound the current comic, not to skip material defects or registration. Freeze scope and story in roughly the first 10 minutes; run independent renderer/geometry, story/saved-audio and necessary artwork lanes in parallel; reserve the final 20 minutes for single-writer integration, one build and the actual changed-reader review. Reuse current good sources and checks, correct observed defects once, and report any exact unresolved output at the deadline. Do not turn this task into a new audit campaign or repeat bulk library checks. Put retained reader screenshots under image-library/records as evidence, never in artwork treatment rows (those require assets paths). Batch production intake as early as its exact inputs allow. On 13 October the real 26,339-file/7.515GB verification still took 14m20s despite valid authenticated hash-cache hits; fixture speedups do not prove a 90-minute whole-issue workflow. Budget that measured guard cost and avoid redundant scans. Design reference: `docs/authoring.md#changes-and-review`; current user scope and canonical registration rules override its older shortcuts.

1. **Freeze the story first** (steps 1-2 above), run `node scripts/comic-story-check.mjs --story src/data/comic-stories/<slug>.ts` and `node scripts/comic-story-deslop.mjs` from `site/`, and record the story sha256. Art requests bind to that hash.
   Check: both commands exit 0 and the hash is written in the request folder.
2. **Write one request row per box or plane** with its exact reference files and sha256 values (cast from `scripts/art/cast_authority.py`, prompt from `comic_art.py box-prompt`, destination passed through `node scripts/assets/check-asset-destination.mjs image <path>`). Record a reference's catalog status; never wait on it.
   Check: every row names its references by path and sha256, and its destination is new.
3. **Generate into transport, never waiting on registration or cast-record state.** A lane is `node .claude/skills/codex-orchestration/scripts/codex_lane.mjs run … --sandbox read-only --timeout-s 560` in the foreground, writing only to `.codex/worktrees/<slug>/wave-NN/lane-<panel>/` and the native cache. Run up to four lanes as parallel tool calls in one message (four ran in about three minutes on 2026-10-12 with about 1 GB free). A running register, a lease on `wiki/image-library/`, or an `unreviewed-preview` sheet does not stop a lane. Attach the new key image (for example the tower master) as a reference in every later lane so the whole issue matches. A tall box needs a portrait source (1024x1536): a landscape image covered into a tall box crops away the subject.
   Check: `receipt.json` lists exactly one image.
3a. **Compose from registered sources and measured reader geometry.** Register every attempt through the named integrating writer before source integration; a transport packet keeps its exact destination and continuing owner until intake succeeds. `comic-box-preview.mjs` is an integration aid, not a completion gate. Its `fillAll` cover shortcut can crop speakers and invalidate balloon coordinates: inspect decoded source dimensions, `ar`, desktop box and every phone sheet, and bind speaker/face anchors to that exact source. Use a purposeful portrait/landscape source or supported alternate view when one composition cannot fill both layouts while preserving faces, acting hands and evidence. Do not set global cover and declare fit passed.
   Check: all actual sources have canonical paths/hashes; inspect each changed scene at complete lettering and its motion sample, on desktop, phone and reduced motion. Home requires at least five distinct, meaningful comic boxes per desktop page and phone sheet. Additional cuts, repeated images or duplicate regions do not increase that count.
3b. **Make animated planes from the master by pixel mask, not by image edits.** An image-edit request to isolate a small object redraws it bigger and shifted (tower plane mean pixel error 56 vs 7.9 for a large foreground, 2026-10-12). Cut what moves (lit floors, a glowing screen, an agent) out of the master with a mask so it stays aligned. Per floor: one RGBA plane each, `layerMotion` `sprout` with `floors`, `floorIndex`, `activeFloors` and `floorSource` bound to the story box that earned that floor. Use image edits only for a clean plate behind an actor that moves.
   Check: the composite of all planes equals the master at the end state.
4. **Make the handoff by script, not by hand.** `lane-manifest.json` comes from the receipt and the request row (Phase 2: `scripts/art/lane_manifest.mjs`). No prose packet, seal, process audit or relocation table inside an art lane.
   Check: the manifest has `output.sha256` and `destination`; `review` and `registration` are the only fields that change later.
5. **Review every returned image at full size** against D1-D15 and the cast sheet (`references/visual-review.md`), and write `review` into the manifest. One correction per observed defect; a second failure goes to Matt with both images.
   Check: no manifest with `selected: true` has an empty `review.checks`.
6. **Register once per wave, as the single writer:** all returned attempts, including rejected art, → `imports.json` → `python .claude/skills/mtw-comic-story/scripts/art/import_native_batch.py --imports <imports.json> --receipt-dir <outside> --check`, then without `--check`. Selection and review remain distinct from registration. The next wave's independent lanes can start while this runs; integration waits for the actual registered binding.
   Check: `wave-manifest.json` records the register and verify receipts and the catalog hash.
7. **Bind and export** the selected masters (`comic_art.py integrate --registered-master …`, 1600-wide, no upscale; story `art`/`layers`/`ar` by compare-and-swap), then `node site/scripts/build-comics-library.mjs`.
   Check: `comic-story-check.mjs --require-art` exits 0.
8. **Score, effects, motion, checks, one build, one browser** (steps 4-5 above, [audio and motion](references/audio-motion.md), [checks](references/checks.md)). Property checks only; never a check pinned to a story timetable.
   Check: frames and mix samples are registered in the image/soundtrack libraries and inspected.
9. **Report one STATUS line per wave**, in the format from the spec, Section 6: lanes done/total, selected, registered with the catalog prefix, pending, next step, manifest path. Then show Matt in Studio.

## Constraints to preserve

- Dark 1980s comic ink: black and teal, flat print colour, hatching and halftone, with depth in shadow planes and movement staged in poses. Reference pages are craft references; our cast, setting and story determine what happens. Do not copy reference characters, marks or words.
- A whole printed page fills the window beneath the MTW header; phones reflow boxes. Thin brand-teal gutters, approved lettering and vector balloons remain. Keep varied box sizes and a dominant beat; equal boxes serve a rapid sequence.
- Every displayed Home desktop page and phone sheet has at least five distinct, meaningful comic boxes. Reflow into complete pages and preserve causal order; splashes, duplicate regions and multiple cuts inside one box do not satisfy the count. Inspect the final sheet after reflow as well as the first. This Home correction does not start production on other comics.
- Speech tails follow the actual speaker in the current crop/view, and all visible faces are protected from accumulated lettering. Use normalized full-source `speakers` mouth/face bounds, updating them for changed compositions and phone alternates; do not carry old box-relative tails across a cover or source change. See [lettering and balloons](references/lettering-and-balloons.md).
- Teal narrative highlights, character introductions and rectangle scene intros use the retained Second Brain voice when matching saved clips exist. Inventory missing clips separately from runtime scheduling; an absent `speech` binding is not repaired by retiming or a caption colour. Follow [saved speech](references/audio-motion.md#optional-saved-speech-on-the-existing-reading-clock).
- Every box is visible at the configured ghost opacity, then fades to full with its balloons. Authored art focus follows [audio and motion](references/audio-motion.md). The current runtime implements push, horizontal drift, light and shadow, plus ordered raster planes and per-plane projection through [scene-layer authoring](references/scene-layers.md). Purposeful multiple-image cuts use optional `panel.views` with box-relative beat offsets and phone/desktop eligibility on the same held story clock. Retain the selected page-art fallback and essential evidence under reduced motion. Other missing effects still need an implemented path. Record exact version/hash/order, readable dwell and clock-driven parameters; keep lettering, borders and paper fixed, and clear of important subjects. Avoid gratuitous effects or interface chrome. The shared motion toolbar controls playback.
- Sound design spans depicted box ambience, physical events, intentional silence and occasional low music; music does not run throughout. Follow [audio and motion](references/audio-motion.md) for the complete scene/closing score, cue lifecycle and bounded panel presets. Keep the user's sound setting, visible controls and navigation audio consistent. Timing leaves enough time to read in order.
- All new art and visual review evidence is versioned and registered in `wiki/image-library/`; the site holds integration copies. Retain earlier artwork and actual review status. A generated file, passing script or style choice is not user approval.
- Keep the current demo unchanged when Matt excludes it. Review new issue work on its own actual routes. Do not force new art into the demo to satisfy an obsolete demo-first instruction.
- Correct a concrete failed beat once, using an observed cause. Repeated prompt wording or score chasing without better storytelling/visibility is waste. If two attempts cannot resolve a real blocker, report the bounded evidence and specific remaining choice; do not run another calibration campaign.
- For a requested demo, prioritize material story/identity/evidence, tower scale, real projection/depth, readable responsive fit and balanced non-startling audio. Preserve minor cosmetic observations as nonblocking review notes and show the actual reviewable issue in Studio; do not generate another version solely to polish those notes. Reuse proven source bytes, composition settings, mix recipes and checks. Human review decides subsequent refinements; do not mark a material defect or an unperformed check passed to move faster.
- Use one browser and one build. Track owned helpers and clean them up. Workers stage exact files externally; the assigned writer integrates.

The original style-lock experiments remain [historical provenance](references/art-pipeline-history.md), not an active queue or gate. Runtime integration checks must enforce content/identity, assets and inspected crop/lettering correctness; they must not require fabricated 95 scores to deliver the accepted style.
