# Audio follows visible opportunities

Matt's latest direction sets a minimum: sound should support at least **50% of the possible audible opportunities shown**. This is a production rubric, not a claim that the current Home reader meets it. Score means the whole sound design: ambience, event effects, deliberate silence, gated voices and occasional earned music. Continuous music cannot earn coverage.

## Freeze the denominator before choosing cues

Inventory **every actual box in order** from the current imported story. Bind the story source hash, exact scene/panel IDs (demo fallback `<scene>:box-<zero-based-index>`), all actual view/master paths and hashes, and observations from looking at those pixels. Alt text, a storyboard or a provider description is not pixel evidence. A changed story/master invalidates that audit.

List distinct supported non-speech opportunities: (1) audible setting/room segments, (2) visibly depicted physical actions/foley, (3) specifically justified digital/learning events. A continuous room segment is one opportunity with all its box anchors, not a new opportunity on each box. A depicted passing streetcar can justify one bounded transit rumble; a later office requires office ambience. A static paper/device or a pose is not proof of a rustle, click, typing or impact. Digital presence alone does not require a noise. Do not add horns, bells, alarms or events that the drawing/story does not support. Keep separate distinct actions only where the image actually supplies them.

Each eligible opportunity counts once. Freeze an ID, category, exact view/box observations and rationale. Repeated cues, louder gain, a longer loop and musical backing cannot increase the numerator. An eligible event intentionally left silent remains in the denominator with its reason and an uncovered decision. A box with no audible opportunity records a quiet reason and contributes zero opportunities; it is not a fulfilled cue. Do not remove supported opportunities to rescue a low result. Human cold review checks whether the inventory is honest; the validator cannot discover omitted sounds or inspect pixels.

Compute `covered / supported opportunities >= 0.50` **per issue**, with counts also shown by category and scene for diagnosis. Zero opportunities is not a quantity pass. There is no additional invented per-scene/category quota. Unavailable effects remain uncovered until suitable reused/source-bound audio exists. Exact human-word/voice/rights-gated speech is inventoried separately and is neither a failed effect nor coverage earned by unauthorized TTS. Keep planned, authored, heard, native-verified and human-approved states separate.

## Bind an authored sound to the visual cause

Use `audio_opportunities.mjs`: `node .codex/skills/mtw-comic-story/scripts/audio_opportunities.mjs --audit <audit.json> --root <repo>` (Claude edition has the identical helper). This reads actual TS stories without browser/build/network and the existing `wiki/soundtrack-library/comic-audio.json` projection. It does not write assets or refresh catalogs. Run `node .../test_audio_opportunities.mjs` for meaningful synthetic failures.

Audit schema 1 has `storyId`, `storySource:{path,sha256}`, `librarySource:{path,sha256}`, ordered `boxes`, `opportunities`, `coverage`, and separate `qualityReview`. A box declares `sceneId,panelId,visualReview:{status,observations},visuals:[{viewId,path,sha256}],decisionReason,opportunityIds:[],speech:[]`. Inspection status is `pending` or `inspected`; pending boxes prevent a quantity pass. Speech entries `{id,status,reason}` use `gated`, `unavailable` or `available-not-counted`.

An opportunity is `{id,category,rationale,anchors:[{sceneId,panelId,viewId,observation}]}`; continuous ambience also has a unique `segmentId`. Coverage has exactly one `{opportunityId,state,reason}` per opportunity. States are `planned`, `silent`, `missing` or `authored`. Authored adds `cue:{libraryRecordId,source,sceneId,panelId,cueIndex?,room?,startBeat,endBeat,gain,fadeInBeats,fadeOutBeats,reuseReason,mixReason}`. `source` is actual `panel-sfx` or `panel-room`; start is the global story beat, not wall-clock time. The helper checks source kind/gain/delay or actual room against the story. It binds real recording hashes or the current procedural function/recipe hash, preserving their non-recorded status. Missing/stale files, unsupported paths, invented/mismatched action, duplicate coverage, music/speech inflation, unsafe levels and uninspected anchors reject fulfilment.

Reuse current soundtrack records first. Legacy comics-library recordings remain compatibility inputs discoverable in that library; future new audio is canonical under soundtrack-library. Never create an MP3 claim for Web Audio recipes. A recorded or procedural cue has explicit local-source/library identity, story-beat alignment, bounded gain/fades, tail/rest and a concrete mix reason. Check real effective levels and overlap, not only 0-1 authored gains. Quiet checking/reading passages should retain useful silence and intelligibility; selective low music may support a dramatic turn. Loud music requires actual characters listening, never a coverage target.

## Hear and verify separately

A 50% count is a minimum quantity result. Listen to every used sound and the actual opening, transition, quiet reading, learning beat, strongest accent and close in desktop/phone playback. Verify depicted onset, duration/tail, room changes, subtle usable dynamics, speech intelligibility, no clipping/stacked loops/fatiguing repetition and musical restraint. Record the exact mixed WebM/source hashes in soundtrack-library; record hearing observations only after listening. Waveform progress/normalization metrics are not listening. Exercise pause/seek/replay/mute/hold/reduced motion through the existing controller/native review and confirm the user's executing story/runtime/art bytes. Offline binding tests do not repair an old live Home page or prove new motion.

## Rebind after measured reader geometry changes

Use paired `scripts/rebind_audio_opportunities.mjs` after page splits, measured art crops, lettering reserves or clock corrections. It reads the retained exact TS source and current exact TS source with the existing auditor, compares globally unique ordered panel IDs, exact words/speakers/caption roles/speech bindings and ordered selected media, and preserves the opportunity IDs, denominator and coverage decisions. Balloon coordinates, tails, off-panel placement and page geometry can change; speech identity cannot. Cue starts/gains come from the actual current panel SFX; one continuous room interval comes from its current anchored panels, never one new opportunity per box. Record the original audit SHA, retained source SHA and original/current library hashes. Reused historical recipe/media records must validate against the current projection; this does not claim the old projection was reloaded. Keep the retained TS beside its exact runtime imports such as `retime.ts`; do not synthesize a missing historical source or call a new source its old hash.
