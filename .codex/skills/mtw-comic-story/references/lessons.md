# Lessons: Matt's corrections, dated, so no agent repeats them

Current separator rule (Matt, 2026-10-09): all frames, gutters, inset bands and page margins use brand teal `#1a9ba1`. The earlier white/cream separator directions below are historical and superseded. Balloon fills and retained reference samples remain independent.

Each line: the date, what Matt said, and the rule now in force. Newest last. The same patterns, with their checks, are in [deslop](deslop.md).

- 2026-09-28 "Villains are announced by the trouble they cause, not by name": no caption, title, NEXT heading or choice names a villain.
- 2026-09-28 "don't use proportional sizing in scenes ... unless it's a rapid few sections": no even grids; one `rapid` scene at most.
- 2026-09-28 "do not use vertical animations. this should be legit comic book": the whole page shows; boxes ghost, then reveal.
- 2026-09-28 "start all boxes at min 30% opacity, and allow the user to click forward into them": ghost opacity; a click on a box jumps to it.
- 2026-09-28 "the 30% is a guess, when artwork arrives that may be 60, make it a constant": `ghostOpacity` in page-constants; `?ghost=` to review.
- 2026-09-28 "no fancy animations on slide fade out": each box fades in reading order, in and out.
- 2026-09-28 "i don't think any of the comics have rough edges for boxes, those are crisp": crisp borders; only `fear` balloons shake.
- 2026-09-28 "do not use generic ovals or squares for speech bubbles": balloons drawn around composed lettering, per `balloon-style.json`.
- 2026-09-28 "some of the chat cloud bubbles look cartoon": shallow, irregular cloud lobes; few small oval beads.
- 2026-09-28 "you can type in speech bubbles too": lettering types on word by word, by opacity, crisp.
- 2026-09-28 "source and download the comic fonts": Shantell Sans (dialogue, bold italic stress) and Bangers (shouts), SIL OFL, self-hosted.
- 2026-09-28 "some transparency in those yellow and other bubbles": fills at `fillAlpha`; ink and lettering opaque.
- 2026-09-28 "if you have styles not used in the comics remove them": no whisper, no machine balloon, no engine overlays.
- 2026-09-28 "it will be the header, this is b real": on a -b page the comic is the header, in flow under the MTW header.
- 2026-09-28 "full screen needs to lock into mtw header and footer and styles": the reader uses BaseLayout.
- 2026-09-28 "use dark by default please" then "margins are all white, that is great ... only the margins between boxes is white, everything else dark": paper only in the gutters between boxes.
- 2026-09-28 "Match the margin colors too ... needs to be minimal" and "use the reference hex": gutters #ffffff (the references' majority), #f8f2e4 the printed alternative.
- 2026-09-28 "ensure margin sizes and width are matched pixel for pixel in source, we look big": gutters, margins and borders are the references' ratios of the short side, whole pixels, within 1 px.
- 2026-09-28 "looking for something sharp": no blur, filters or rasterizing; geometry snapped to whole pixels.
- 2026-09-28 "visually verify you are in the style boundary first ... constantly improve the skill": the style lab and style check come before any layout or art; this skill is updated with every correction.
- 2026-09-28 "if we are overlaying a box on top of other boxes, we need to ensure the margin is reconstructed" then "it is ok to overlap boxes in the middle but the margins need to fill same color": an overlapping box (inset) keeps a full gutter band of paper around it, at full strength, then its black border; it may span boxes in the middle of the page, never touch the outer edge.
- 2026-09-28 "refine styles at bottom to reuse the styles from 8 bit for the buttons, we have invented new styles": the controls are the canonical motion toolbar (MotionToolbar.astro); comic-story.css styles no buttons.
- 2026-09-28 "add an anti-invention de-slop to the skill": every visual element needs a citation in assets/style-provenance.json or a dated exception; the lint fails "invented: <x>".
- 2026-09-28 "imo it is good to use many small boxes if you can animate the character via quick intros": runs (`run` on boxes) step a character through a moment in quick equal boxes; equal tiers are allowed only as a run.
- 2026-09-28 "the oval chat bubbles have an obvious overlay opacity issue in their stems. you will need to improve visual verification": a balloon's paper (body, tail, neck) is one group with one opacity, never stacked translucent shapes; the style check's seam test fails any fill that varies over 2 levels on flat grey.
- 2026-09-28 "use the correct chat bubble style depending on if it is people, chat, spooked, etc.": a balloon's `state` decides its kind (the usage table); the checker fails a mismatch.
- 2026-09-29 "make the margin look like paper, not bright white" / "ensure paper texture": the whole page is printed paper (`#f8f2e4`) with one multiplied texture tile sampled from a photographed page, inside a thin dark band.
- 2026-09-29 hi-res image art: generated pages ship as AVIF/WebP at 2x or more through srcset; only page art is raster, everything lettered or ruled stays vector.
- 2026-09-29 "add a loader so you can preload all of these": a printed-page loader with honest progress; a box never reveals before its art is decoded.
- 2026-09-29 (root, measured) "no reference has cloud thought balloons": the measured usage table (assets/reference-balloons/report.md) decides every balloon kind; no clouds; the fear balloon is scalloped (the Avengers page), panic is angular bursts, a weak voice has a zigzag tail, menace a red ragged second outline, off-panel a long thin tail; `state` names the voice and the checker fails a mismatch.
- 2026-09-29 phone captions broke words ("WEDNES DAY"): the engine composes every line itself (never the browser's wrap, never a broken word); a caption that cannot fit its cascade inset grows the inset (up to 1.5x) or moves to the host box's first corner that no inset covers, reading after the host's own caption.
- 2026-09-29 "i want the speech bubbles to match the reference images. i think you invented the color change": never re-tint a measured element to match another surface. Balloons are the measured #f4f4f4; the page paper #f8f2e4 is for margins and gutters only. Lint: balloon fill must equal the measured value.
- 2026-09-29 "i want the comic scenes to look like paper, so if you need to add texture do it": the paper texture multiplies over the page's art, margins and gutters and inside each box's art; balloons and captions carry a mean-preserving grain of their own (their mean stays measured within 1 level; the style check's mean test). A multiply layer over an empty, transparent box turned it white: the art layer carries the box's dark base.
- 2026-09-29 "chat bubbles have regressed significantly" and "we need to tighten up significantly they look cartoon ... agreed too big": the references' "1.2% of the page width" is of a portrait page, i.e. its short side; tight padding (0.6c), squarer bodies (n 4.5), thin 0.2c outlines; a/b kept by composing lines, never by inflating the body (inflating made fat pillows).
- 2026-09-29 "don't make too small" and "make yellow square text bigger": dialogue at least 15 px on desktop, 14 px on a phone; captions at the dialogue size. Tighten with padding, shape and stroke, never by shrinking type below the floor.
- 2026-09-29 "make more savage and tight": the low end of the measured padding range, squarer Marvel bodies, sharper deeper burst teeth, all inside the measured ranges.
- 2026-09-29 "we need to make the white bubbles standard. we can have yellow bubbles, and that can indicate something like thinking, the stem of the bubble should standardize that": white #f4f4f4 with a wedge tail for every voice; the yellow balloon (#ede614) means thinking and always has the bead stem; captions are yellow squares. Lint: yellow balloon without beads, or beads on white, fails.
- 2026-09-29 "looks perfect. standardize and update skill craft so this is reproducible": the standard is frozen (balloon-style.json 2.0.0, `approved`) with a golden reference (assets/golden/, scripts/comic-story-golden.mjs). A change needs Matt's approval, a new version and a re-captured golden.
- 2026-09-29 (engine) the analysis crops carry tracing overlays: Codex read a printed red ring as a tracing guide. Reference sheets for Codex are cut from the original pages (comic-story-style-refs.json boxes), never the annotated crops.
- 2026-09-28 "my face and form should fit into the other characters. don't overdo that": Matt is drawn at the same ink economy and detail level as the rest of the cast, never more rendered, photographic or traced. His photo is an identity input only, and the likeness check scores style parity with the cast beside likeness ([characters](characters.md)).
- 2026-09-30 "Yes, refine to 95" and "Kalam lettering": Kalam 700 for dialogue and captions (its stress slanted further and heavier, Kalam has no italic); fear lobes sized by width so they read all round; a slim two-bend weak tail; a tapered across connector; a little more vertical padding; bursts wide with many short teeth, tuned by the silhouette IoU (searched offline with the style check's own metric, then confirmed in the lab). A synthetic `oblique` did not render on Kalam in Chrome; `italic` with a same-ink stroke did.
- 2026-09-30 an off-panel voice cannot pass the IoU floor against short-tailed speech outlines without breaking the recipe's long tail: it is reported, not gated, until a single off-panel reference outline is measured.
- 2026-10-02 "Accept 2.1.0": the balloon standard 2.1.0 is accepted at Codex 94 (style check 43) as the baseline; golden 2.1.0 is the regression reference. No further balloon tuning without Matt; Codex's later targets oscillated (outline weight, connector width, weak-tail shape), so chasing them round after round is not a reason to change the accepted standard.
- Historical style-campaign rule, retired by Matt’s 2026-10-04 acceptance and 2026-10-08 demo exclusion: 2026-10-02 "i cannot give you any feedback until a demo is created" / "if you are not making 5-10% gains on every iteration then you are doing this wrong": `/comics/demo/` plays the best art of each kind in the engine at all times; a round that gains under 5 points retires its lever; two failed levers in a row stop the loop for Matt's call on the demo. The demo's box regions come from the page art's paper gutters (recursive XY cut).
