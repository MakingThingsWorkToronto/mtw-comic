## Read-only live reader geometry

Use `window.MTWComicStory.geometry()` for an editor or inspection overlay to query the actual current reader, rather than projecting story percentages over an assumed page. Its `coordinateSpace: document-css-px` rectangles use live `getBoundingClientRect()` plus the reader document’s scroll offsets, without DPR multiplication. `rect` is the full DOM bounding rectangle; `visibleRect` intersects the viewport and ancestors’ axis-aligned overflow clips. The snapshot contains story/current clock scene/beat/seconds, mode/viewport, visible page/panel/balloon IDs, kind and authored speaker; it contains no words or playback controls. Balloon IDs match the comics producer’s text-review row IDs. Reparented phone captions retain their source identity while reporting their actual visible host geometry. Reduced-motion pages are enumerated by live DOM visibility rather than assumed current-clock layout. Rectangles do not measure polygon shape, text-ink overlap, image subject coverage or occlusion by unrelated overlays; they do not replace the strongest `comic-visual-v1` review.

A same-origin parent can send `{type:'mtw-geometry',requestId}` and receives `{type:'mtw-geometry-result',requestId,geometry}` at that exact origin. Cross-origin and opaque (`null`) origins receive no response; a file reader can use the direct SDK. The request is read-only, never seeks, resumes, changes words or edits browser drafts.
